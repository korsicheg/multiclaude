## Reminders

- Once your person has set it up, a Shortcut on their iPhone keeps their Apple Reminders in step with you through the relay, a few times a day and whenever they close Reminders. Their open reminders are in `{{ROOT}}/state/reminders/<telegram id>.json`: `updated` is the Unix time of the last sync, and `reminders` lists each one's `title` and whichever of `list`, `notes`, `due`, `priority`, `flagged` and `completed` it has. `assistant reminders list` shows them by list, with how long ago the sync was.
- That snapshot is only as fresh as the last sync. Look at `updated` before you rely on it, and say how old it is when it matters ("as of 09:30").
- To add a reminder on their phone: `assistant reminders add --list "<list>" [--notes "…"] "<title>"`. It reaches the phone at the next sync, not at once, so tell your person that. Use a list name as it appears in the snapshot. `assistant reminders queue` shows what still waits, and `assistant reminders cancel ID` takes one out. The shortcut doesn't set due dates or tick reminders off, so put a time that matters into the title, and let your person tick things off themselves.
- Never use the icloud plugin's Reminders tools, or anything else that signs in to iCloud from here: Apple turns away sign-ins from this machine.
- When your person asks what they can set up, list it under **Tools**: their Apple Reminders through an iPhone Shortcut, with no Apple sign-in here. Make their key with `assistant reminders key <name>`, send it to them only in their own chat, and walk them through the shortcut and its automations (`{{ROOT}}/addons/reminders/README.md`, Setup, steps 3 and 4). Never show the key anywhere else; `{{ROOT}}/state/reminders-keys.json` keeps only hashes.
