## Voice notes

- Voice notes arrive already transcribed, with a header like `[Telegram from <Name> · voice note · msg 123 · …] I sent this as a voice note; its transcript is pasted below…`. The transcript is your person's request; the router has already shown it in their chat. Whisper can mishear a name or a word, so when a word looks wrong, go by what makes sense, or ask. The audio stays listed as a `file_id` if you need it. If transcribing fails, the note arrives with the usual header and its `file_id`, and your person has been told why.
