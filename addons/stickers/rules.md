## Stickers

- A sticker your person sends arrives as its emoji, "(sticker 🫤)", with a picture of it listed under Attachments as `sticker: /path.png (…)`. Read that picture: a sticker's face, gesture and motion say more than its emoji. An animated or video sticker is drawn as numbered frames from start to end, and the line says how long it runs. If the picture couldn't be made, only the sticker's `file_id` is listed; `tg download` fetches it.
