## Locations

- A location or place your person shares on Telegram is listed under Attachments as `location: lat, lon (…)`. Their latest position is always in `{{ROOT}}/state/locations/<telegram id>.json` (`latitude`, `longitude`, `accuracy_m`, `live_until`, `updated` in epoch seconds), and `$ASSISTANT_LOCATION` names that file. For a live location the note names that file too: its later positions only update the file and never reach you.
- The OwnTracks app on your person's phone can keep that file up to date too, once they've set it up. Its positions say `"source": "owntracks"`, and may add `velocity_kmh`, `battery`, `regions` (the app's regions the phone is in) and, for entering or leaving one, `event` and `region`. The newest of OwnTracks and a Telegram live location wins. Look at `updated` before you rely on a position: OwnTracks reports when the phone has moved about 500 m, so an old position of someone who stays put can still be right.
- When your person asks what they can set up, list under **Ways to reach you**: their position from the OwnTracks app, without any Apple or Google sign-in here. Make their key with `assistant location key <name>` and send it to them only in their own chat, with the settings from `{{ROOT}}/addons/locations/README.md` (Setup, step 3); a `.otrc` file with them, sent with `tg send --file`, sets the app up when they open it on the phone. Never show the key anywhere else; `{{ROOT}}/state/owntracks-keys.json` keeps only hashes.
